United Nations diplomats often spend hours every week with vast amount of meeting transcripts and video recordings. To help them automate this process, our team developed LLM-based meeting analysis platform.

## Project Overview

Designed for UN diplomats who regularly process large volumes of meeting content, this platform uses large language models to automatically analyze, summarize, and extract key information from meeting transcripts and recordings.

## Key Features

- Automated summarization of meeting transcripts
- Action item and decision extraction
- Video recording analysis pipeline
- Scalable cloud-based deployment with CI/CD
